## Top 10 for a synth-time rule
Ranking = real-incident frequency x template visibility x fit with ProofGate's finite path-to-protected-resource model.

1. **Code/config-update path to a stronger role without PassRole** (#1). Seen in the 2025 Sysdig case; fits the model; already planned as `implicit-role-paths`.
2. **IAM self-escalation action set** (#2, #10): CreateRole/AttachRolePolicy/PutRolePolicy/CreatePolicyVersion/CreateAccessKey/UpdateAssumeRolePolicy on `*`. Appears in both Unit 42 and Sysdig; trivial to see.
3. **Wildcard PassRole + launch action** (#12). Direct extension of PG-IAM-004 and matches the cryptomining campaigns.
4. **Public protected bucket** (#6): bucket policy Principal `*` or missing BlockPublicAccess. Very frequent; one more Boolean fact.
5. **Write access to protected bucket with no SSE-C deny / versioning** (#5). Same reachability fact as PG-IAM-004's S3 part.
6. **Instance-profile role reaching protected bucket with IMDSv1 allowed** (#3). Capital One; visible via `MetadataOptions`; needs EC2 modeling.
7. **GitHub OIDC trust without `sub` pin** (#7). Well documented. Needs `Condition` support, today rejected.
8. **Foreign-account or `*` trust without ExternalId/OrgID** (#8). Common in vendor integrations; needs account principals and conditions.
9. **Unauthenticated Cognito role reaching protected bucket** (#11). Visible; research-backed, no named victim.
10. **Lambda function URL with AuthType NONE on a role that reaches protected bucket** (#9). Visible; evidence is attacker-side, so lower frequency.

Excluded as not template-visible: leaked keys, SSRF, console takeover, IdP compromise (#4 partly: only the existence of IAM users/keys).
