## Analysis

**Where ProofGate has a unique role (relational properties).**
- Item 10 (least privilege review) and item 12 (IAM policy generation pipeline) are the closest matches. The pages talk about reviewing wildcards and gating policies before creation. Wildcard checks are covered by cdk-nag IAM5 and Checkov CKV_AWS_62/63. They look at one policy at a time. Checkov CKV_AWS_110 (privilege escalation) is also per policy. PG-IAM-004 is different: it joins a principal's PassRole and launch actions, a target role's trust policy and its S3 access, and a protected bucket declared by the app team. No tool in the two indexes I read expresses that join.
- Item 11 (data perimeters) is the largest unserved space. The page is about trusted identities reaching trusted resources. A template-time version is "which roles in this app can reach this protected bucket, by which path". ProofGate's protected-bucket input already matches this. It would need Condition support (aws:PrincipalOrgID, aws:SourceVpce), which is a PG-IAM-003 rejection today. Real org-wide SCP/RCP perimeters are outside what a template shows.
- Item 15 (data security posture) fits in the "declare crown jewels in code" form. Discovery stays with Macie.
- Item 24 (SSM management) and item 21 (network segmentation) are relational but weak fits. Treat as later candidates, not core.

**Better left to cdk-nag / Checkov / Access Analyzer.**
- Single-resource properties: IMDSv2 (9), block public access (14), encryption at rest and in transit (16, 18), risky ports and SGs (22, 23), Lambda URL auth (27), Cognito MFA (3), backups (17), literal secrets (19). Checkov covers all of these with CloudFormation checks. cdk-nag AwsSolutions covers most (S2, S10, RDS2, EC26, EFS1, SQS2, EC23, COG2, RDS13, DDB3) but has no IMDSv2 rule in the AwsSolutions pack; only the HIPAA and NIST packs include EC2IMDSv2Enabled. Writing these in ProofGate would duplicate existing tools and dilute the Lean/fail-closed story.
- Unused/external access (6) needs Access Analyzer. Root, MFA for humans, federation setup, temporary elevated access, multi-account, SCPs (1 partial) are account or runtime state with no CloudFormation signal.

**Phase support.** ProofGate's rules sit in Phase 3 (Efficient: least privilege review, IaC, DevSecOps) and Phase 4 (Optimized: data perimeters, IAM policy generation pipeline). They do not help Phase 1 quick wins, which are mostly account settings or single-resource flags. A pitch that stays accurate: "a pre-deploy gate for the IAM Policy Generation Pipeline and Least Privilege Review items, with a first step toward data perimeters". Do not claim coverage of the Phase 1 or 2 items. The Lean result checks the generated finite model only (per docs/ASSUMPTIONS.md); the Maturity Model pages name no tool, so any mapping here is my reading, not an AWS statement.

**Caveats.** The English model page gives the item list but the phase of each item is read from the site's left navigation. I read the Checkov and cdk-nag indexes by grep, so "no rule found" means not found in those files at the time fetched (cdk-nag main, Checkov main). I did not verify Checkov's IMDS coverage for AWS::EC2::Instance.
